# Data Deletion Requests — The Minister's Term

This page explains how to request deletion of data associated with
*The Minister's Term* by **Yury Zayets (yzapps)**.

## How to request deletion

1. Send an email to **yzapps.dev@gmail.com** with the subject line
   **"Data deletion request — The Minister's Term"**.
2. If possible, include your device model and the approximate period you
   played — the Game has **no accounts**, so this helps me locate any
   matching records.
3. We will confirm and complete the request within **30 days**.

## What data is deleted or kept

- **Game saves, progress, and settings** are stored only on your device and
  are deleted when you uninstall the Game — we never receive them.
- **Anonymous analytics, crash reports, and gameplay-replay records**
  (see our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/Yury-Zayets/ministersterm-privacy))
  are keyed to a random app-instance identifier, **not to your identity**.
  On request we delete records matching the identifiers you can provide;
  uninstalling the Game also disconnects your device from any future data.
- **Aggregated statistics** that can no longer be linked to any individual
  or device may be retained (e.g. overall difficulty win rates).

No account exists to close, and no personal information (name, email,
contacts, location) is collected in the first place.
